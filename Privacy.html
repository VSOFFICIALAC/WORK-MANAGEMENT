Workbench privacy

Workbench uses the email address you verify and stores your tasks, projects, and meetings in a separate Google spreadsheet owned by the app operator. The spreadsheet is not shared with you directly; your workspace is accessed through the Workbench app.

Your password is stored as a salted, iterated cryptographic hash. Verification codes and signed-in sessions expire. The app does not request your Google Drive, Gmail, or Calendar permissions and does not read your inbox.

Task and meeting reminder emails are sent to your verified email address from the Google account that operates Workbench. Google Apps Script and Google Mail quotas may limit service availability.

Signing in to a Google account is required to open this Apps Script deployment. That is separate from your Workbench email and password; the app uses its operator's Google permissions to access its private storage and send email.

To request deletion of your account and its data, contact the Workbench operator.
